What This Simulator Is

The Sextant Protocol DP Resilience Cockpit is a deterministic research and engineering simulation environment for studying how a layered resilience architecture could respond to changing maritime environmental conditions.

The simulator creates a controlled virtual vessel environment in which wind, current, wave and tidal conditions can be varied, repeated and analysed. Each scenario is processed by the deterministic simulation engine and presented through the cockpit for technical observation.

ENVIRONMENT → PRIMARY AI → SECONDARY AI → STABILIZER → HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP → TRIAL MANOEUVRE → SIMULATED DP RESPONSE

The research environment is intended to allow engineers, maritime professionals, researchers and reviewers to examine how the proposed architecture behaves as simulated environmental stress changes.

In particular, the simulator provides a controlled environment for investigating:

  • deterministic environmental scenario generation;
  • environmental stress calculation and risk classification;
  • independent Primary AI and Secondary AI assessments;
  • deterministic resilience arbitration through the Stabilizer;
  • early recognition of simulated deteriorating conditions;
  • operator decision-support and escalation recommendations;
  • trial manoeuvre generation and verification;
  • human-in-the-loop authority and intervention points;
  • simulated DP response behaviour;
  • repeatable scenario testing and comparison;
  • audit and event recording; and
  • future validation through software testing, hardware-in-the-loop simulation and independent engineering review.

The simulator is therefore intended to answer a research question: can a layered deterministic resilience architecture provide earlier recognition, structured escalation and improved decision-support during rapidly changing simulated maritime conditions?

This prototype provides a controlled environment in which that hypothesis can be demonstrated, challenged, measured and independently evaluated before any consideration of physical implementation.

Research Purpose & Operational Boundary

SEXTANT PROTOCOL — DP RESILIENCE RESEARCH SIMULATOR

This cockpit is a deterministic research and engineering simulation environment developed to explore how a layered resilience architecture could respond to changing maritime environmental conditions.

The simulator creates a controlled virtual vessel environment in which wind, current, wave and tidal conditions can be varied, repeated and analysed. The deterministic simulation engine processes those conditions through the research architecture and produces a traceable simulated result.

The simulator is intended to support research, education, engineering analysis, scenario testing, technical demonstration, repeatable validation and future independent review.

Trial manoeuvres shown in this cockpit are simulated planning and assessment representations. They are not operational DP commands and are not transmitted to any vessel control system.

The simulated outputs can be used to examine relationships between environmental loading, resilience assessment, arbitration, operator attention, trial manoeuvre planning and simulated response.

HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP

Human authority remains the final decision point in the research architecture. The simulator provides decision-support and simulated responses; it does not replace the Master, DPO, authorised operator, vessel procedures or classification requirements.

OPERATIONAL BOUNDARY

The prototype is intentionally isolated from operational vessel control. It does not connect to and must not be connected to:

  • Dynamic Positioning systems
  • Propulsion systems
  • Navigation systems
  • Steering systems
  • Safety systems
  • Vessel automation systems

It is not certified marine control software and does not provide certified DP functionality.

All environmental conditions, vessel behaviour, sensor states, AI assessments, stabilizer decisions, trial manoeuvres, alerts and simulated actions displayed by this cockpit are non-operational research representations generated by the simulation environment.
