Research Purpose & Operational Boundary

SEXTANT PROTOCOL — DP RESILIENCE RESEARCH SIMULATOR

This cockpit is a deterministic research and engineering simulation environment developed to explore how a layered resilience architecture could respond to changing maritime environmental conditions.

The simulator creates a controlled virtual vessel environment in which wind, current, wave and tidal conditions can be varied, repeated and analysed.

The deterministic simulation engine processes those conditions through the research architecture and produces a traceable simulated result.

The simulator is intended to support:
• research
• education
• engineering analysis
• scenario testing
• technical demonstration
• repeatable validation
• future independent review

Trial manoeuvres and simulated responses shown by this cockpit are planning and assessment representations only.

They are NOT operational DP commands and are NOT transmitted to any vessel control system.

HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP

Human authority remains the final decision point in the research architecture.

The simulator provides decision-support and simulated responses; it does not replace the Master, DPO, authorised operator, vessel procedures or classification requirements.

OPERATIONAL BOUNDARY

This prototype is intentionally isolated from operational vessel control.

It does not connect to and must not be connected to:
• Dynamic Positioning systems
• Propulsion systems
• Navigation systems
• Steering systems
• Safety systems
• Vessel automation systems

It is not certified marine control software and does not provide certified DP functionality.
