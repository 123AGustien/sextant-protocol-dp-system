Research Scope

This publication describes a deterministic research and engineering simulation environment intended to investigate:

- environmental scenario generation;
- environmental stress assessment;
- independent analytical layers;
- resilience arbitration;
- cascade recognition;
- operator decision-support;
- human-in-the-loop authority;
- simulated DP response;
- repeatable testing;
- auditability; and
- future validation pathways.

The publication does not constitute evidence of operational safety, certification, class approval or suitability for deployment on a vessel.

---

Research Status

The work described in this publication represents a research hypothesis and simulation prototype.

The research proposition is intended to be:

TESTED → CHALLENGED → MEASURED → REPEATED → INDEPENDENTLY REVIEWED

Future research may include software verification, fault injection, baseline comparison, human-factors assessment, higher-fidelity simulation, hardware-in-the-loop testing and independent technical review.

---

Intellectual and Technical Position

Sextant Protocol™ is presented as a research architecture for investigation and discussion.

No classification society, regulator, professional organisation, vessel operator, manufacturer or other third party is represented as having reviewed, approved, certified or endorsed the system unless such review or endorsement is separately documented.

---

Operational Boundary

The simulator is intentionally isolated from operational vessel systems.

It is not intended to connect directly to:

- Dynamic Positioning systems;
- propulsion systems;
- navigation systems;
- steering systems;
- safety systems; or
- vessel automation systems.

The simulator does not issue operational commands to a real vessel.

Human authority remains final within the current research simulation architecture.

---

Future Research

Future research may investigate whether the resilience architecture can be extended to other domains, including maritime, aviation, terrestrial transport, energy, cybersecurity, financial resilience and orbital systems.

Any transition toward higher-assurance or autonomous implementation would require separate engineering validation, safety assurance, cybersecurity assessment, human-factors evaluation, regulatory consideration and independent verification.
