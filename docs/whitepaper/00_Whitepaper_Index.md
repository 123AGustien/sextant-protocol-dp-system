Research Proposition

The central proposition of this research is:

«A layered resilience architecture combining independent assessment, deterministic arbitration and structured human decision-support may provide earlier recognition and more consistent escalation of developing multi-factor conditions in a controlled simulation environment.»

This remains a hypothesis.

The simulator exists to allow that hypothesis to be:

TESTED

CHALLENGED

MEASURED

REPEATED

INDEPENDENTLY REVIEWED

---

What the Simulator Does

The simulator provides a controlled research environment for investigating:

- environmental scenario generation;
- environmental stress;
- independent Primary and Secondary assessment;
- deterministic resilience arbitration;
- early-warning states;
- operator decision-support;
- escalation recommendations;
- simulated DP response;
- event recording;
- auditability;
- repeatability;
- and future fault-injection research.

---

What the Simulator Does Not Do

The current prototype does not:

- control a real vessel;
- issue operational DP commands;
- connect to a vessel DP system;
- connect to propulsion;
- connect to navigation;
- connect to steering;
- connect to safety systems;
- provide certified DP functionality;
- or replace the Master, DPO or authorised operator.

---

Human Authority

The current research architecture deliberately maintains:

HUMAN AUTHORITY = FINAL

The simulator provides assessment and decision-support.

The human operator remains responsible for the final simulated decision.

---

Cascade Research Principle

The research is particularly interested in the question of whether developing conditions can be recognised before they become a larger cascading event.

Conceptually:

ENVIRONMENTAL CHANGE

↓

SYSTEM DEMAND

↓

REDUCED MARGIN

↓

RESILIENCE CONCERN

↓

OPERATOR ATTENTION

↓

ESCALATION / RESPONSE

The Stabilizer is intended to provide a structured arbitration layer rather than simply allowing independent assessment states to propagate without control.
