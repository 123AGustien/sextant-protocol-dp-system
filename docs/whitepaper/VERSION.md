Research Baseline

Version 1.0 establishes the following research components:

- deterministic environmental scenario generation;
- environmental stress assessment;
- Primary AI assessment layer;
- independent Secondary AI safety layer;
- deterministic Stabilizer arbitration;
- resilience early-warning states;
- structured operator decision-support;
- human-in-the-loop decision authority;
- simulated DP response;
- event and audit recording;
- controlled scenario repetition; and
- defined operational separation from real vessel systems.

---

Stabilizer Research Objective

The Stabilizer is not presented as a mechanism that independently "solves" a vessel emergency.

Its research purpose is to investigate whether a deterministic arbitration layer can:

1. recognise developing or conflicting conditions;
2. prevent uncontrolled propagation of assessment states;
3. classify the resulting resilience condition;
4. structure an appropriate escalation level;
5. generate clear operator decision-support; and
6. provide the human operator with sufficient information to make the final decision.

The central research interest is therefore cascade management and decision-support, rather than autonomous replacement of the vessel's existing control architecture.

---

Human Authority

Version 1.0 maintains:

HUMAN AUTHORITY = FINAL

The simulator does not transfer operational authority to the AI layers.

The Primary AI, Secondary AI and Stabilizer are research components providing simulated assessment, arbitration and decision-support.

---

Autonomous Research Direction

Future Sextant Protocol™ research may investigate progressively higher levels of automation, including the wider Captain AI Lena research concept.

Any such development remains outside the operational scope of Version 1.0.

A future autonomous architecture would require separate:

- safety assurance;
- engineering validation;
- software verification;
- cybersecurity assessment;
- human-factors evaluation;
- failure-mode analysis;
- regulatory review; and
- independent verification and validation.

No autonomous operational claim is made by this release.

Release Integrity Statement

Version 1.0 is a research baseline.

It should not be interpreted as:

- marine control software certification;
- class approval;
- regulatory approval;
- operational DP approval;
- autonomous vessel approval;
- guaranteed safety;
- or evidence that the system is suitable for deployment on a vessel.

The purpose of this release is to make the research concept visible, testable, auditable and independently challengeable.
