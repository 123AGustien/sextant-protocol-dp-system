# Sextant Protocol™ – DP Resilience Research White Paper

Welcome to the official white paper for the **Sextant Protocol™ – DP Resilience Research Simulator**.

This directory contains the technical documentation supporting the Sextant Protocol™ Dynamic Positioning (DP) Resilience Research Simulator.

The purpose of this white paper is to present the research motivation, deterministic resilience architecture, simulation methodology, case studies, operational boundaries, and proposed validation roadmap.

The simulator is a research and engineering prototype intended for simulation, education, technical analysis, and independent review.

**Operational Boundary**

- Research and simulation only.
- Not certified marine control software.
- No connection to operational vessel systems.
- Human authority remains final.
