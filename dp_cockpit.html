Research Purpose

This cockpit is a deterministic maritime resilience research simulator developed for V&V evaluation.

It is designed to examine system behaviour under changing environmental and operational disturbance conditions.

The simulator provides decision support and records the relationship between environmental conditions, positioning configuration, engine-load configuration, AI assessment, stabilizer arbitration, reserve-power verification, recommended actions, human authorization and simulated response.

Operational Boundary

RESEARCH / V&V ONLY

This system is a simulation and research instrument.

No autonomous vessel control is permitted.
