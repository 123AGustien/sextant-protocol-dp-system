🧭 The Stabilizer

The Stabilizer is a central research component of the architecture.

Its purpose is not to independently "solve" a vessel emergency.

The research objective is to investigate whether deterministic arbitration can help:

1. recognise developing or conflicting conditions;
2. control propagation of assessment states;
3. classify the resilience condition;
4. structure escalation;
5. provide clear operator actions; and
6. preserve final human decision authority.

The research therefore focuses on cascade management and decision-support, rather than replacing conventional vessel control systems.

Verification and Validation

The current catalogue provides a research and engineering framework for repeatable scenario testing.

Verification addresses whether the simulator implements the specified behaviour correctly, including:

- input processing;
- environmental stress calculation;
- risk classification;
- layer sequencing;
- stabilizer arbitration;
- human authorization gating;
- simulated response handling; and
- audit recording.

Validation addresses whether the research implementation appropriately addresses the intended engineering purpose and test objective.

The catalogue therefore does not claim formal marine-system certification or independent validation.
